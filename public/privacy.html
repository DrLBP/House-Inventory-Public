Home Inventory - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Home Inventory is a private, personal app used by a single household to document its own belongings for insurance purposes. It is not offered to the public, and new accounts cannot be created.

What the app stores

  • Item details the household enters (names, categories, brands, model and serial numbers, purchase dates and prices, estimated values, notes).
  • Small preview images, stored privately with our database provider (Supabase).
  • Full-size photos, receipts and a backup spreadsheet, stored in the household's own Google Drive.

Google data

The app requests only the drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files and folders it created. It cannot see any other files in Google Drive. It also reads the connected Google account's email address, only to display which account is connected.

Google data is used only to store and retrieve the household's own inventory files. It is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone, and it is not used for advertising or any other purpose. Files are never made public or shared by link. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The Google access key is stored encrypted and is never sent to the phone. Access can be removed at any time from the app's Settings screen or from Google Account permissions. Disconnecting does not delete files already in Google Drive.

AI assist (optional)

Only when the household taps an "AI assist" button, the chosen item's photos and receipts (which may come from its Google Drive folder) and its current details are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to suggest details or look up a current price. The household approves every suggestion before anything is saved. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models by default. Nothing is sent to Anthropic unless the button is tapped, and Google data is never used to train any AI model.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties, except as described under "AI assist" above. Reports and exports are created on the device and downloaded only to that device.

Contact

This app is operated by its owner for personal use. Questions can be sent to the support email listed on the Google sign-in screen.
